### 👨‍💻 About Me

Passionate engineering student specializing in **IoT, Robotics, Edge AI, and Embedded Systems** at **University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB)**. I bridge the gap between low-level hardware design and high-level artificial intelligence—creating end-to-end intelligent systems, digital twins, and autonomous solutions.

- 🌐 **Live Web Portfolio**: [**rakibdipu.github.io**](https://rakibdipu.github.io)
- 🔭 **Currently Building**: Edge AI systems, Structural Health Monitoring Digital Twins, and WiFi CSI Spatial Localization.
- 🔬 **Research Focus**: IoT-driven healthcare solutions, wearable panic detection, RTOS schedulers, and embedded machine learning.
- 📜 **Published Papers**: 
  - First-author IEEE paper on IoT-based panic attack detection for Autism Spectrum Disorder (*RAAICON 2025* on [**IEEE Xplore**](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11502258)).
  - RTOS-Based Intelligent Railway Crossing System using ESP32-S3 (*ECCT 2026 / Taylor & Francis Books*).
- 🏆 **Awards & Leadership**: **ImpactX 2026 Finalist**, Deputy Organizing Secretary at **UFTB Robotics Club** & 1st Runner-Up at **Hult Prize Campus Competition 2025**.
- 💬 **Ask Me About**: `ESP32-S3 / FreeRTOS`, `WiFi CSI Spatial Sensing`, `ROS / Robotics`, `Machine Learning & NLP`, `PCB Layout (Altium / EasyEDA)`, and `IoT Cloud Telemetry`.
- 📬 **Get in Touch**: Reach me directly via [Email](mailto:rakibdipu007@gmail.com) or [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/rakibhassandipu).
