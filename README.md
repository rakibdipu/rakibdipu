## 🙋‍♂️ Who Am I?

> *"I build intelligence into hardware — from PCB traces to neural networks."*

I'm an **IoT & Robotics Engineering student** at the intersection of **hardware and artificial intelligence**. My work spans wearable health systems, digital twins, WiFi-based localization, edge ML deployment, and PCB design — all focused on solving real human problems with elegant engineering.

- 🔭 **Now Working On** → Bridge Structural Health Monitoring · WiFi CSI Indoor Localization · Edge AI deployment on ESP32-S3  
- 🔬 **Research Interests** → Embedded ML · IoT Healthcare · Human Presence Detection · Digital Twin Systems  
- 📄 **Published** → First-author IEEE paper @ **RAAICON 2025** (panic attack detection for autism spectrum disorder)  
- 🏆 **Recognized** → 1st Runner-Up, Hult Prize Campus 2025 · Deputy Organizing Secretary, UFTB Robotics Club  
- 📬 **Reach Me** → [rakibdipu007@gmail.com](mailto:rakibdipu007@gmail.com) · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/rakibhassandipu)
